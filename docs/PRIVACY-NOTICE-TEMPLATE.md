# Privacy Notice Template

This template must be reviewed for every launch jurisdiction before publication.

AI HR processes account identity, HR records, uploaded company documents, workflow history, decisions, and audit events to provide HR operations and decision-support services. The deploying organization is responsible for identifying its lawful basis, defining retention periods, responding to data-subject requests, managing subprocessors, and telling workers how AI is used.

AI HR must not be used to infer protected characteristics or create individual attrition, hiring, promotion, compensation, discipline, or termination scores. High-impact outputs are advisory and require accountable human review.

Before publication, add the legal entity, controller/processor roles, contact details, jurisdictions, subprocessors, transfer mechanisms, retention schedule, individual rights, complaint route, and effective date.
