# AI Impact Assessment

## Intended Use

Draft HR artifacts, retrieve approved company knowledge, calculate aggregate workforce statistics, and prepare evidence-based decision briefs.

## Prohibited Use

- Sole or fully automated hiring, rejection, promotion, compensation, discipline, leave, or termination decisions.
- Individual attrition or flight-risk scoring.
- Protected-characteristic inference, ranking, or proxy discrimination.
- Unsupported causal claims from observational workforce data.

## Controls

- Deterministic aggregate analytics and visible provenance.
- Unsupported-percentage, causal-language, and profiling warnings.
- Protected fields excluded from analytics imports.
- Human approve/reject gates and immutable audit events.
- Role access, MFA, privacy export, deletion, retention, backup, and incident controls.

## Required Assessment

Before real use, document affected people, jurisdictions, lawful basis, necessity, alternatives, data quality, bias testing, accessibility, explanation and appeal paths, human reviewer authority, monitoring metrics, incident response, and retirement criteria.
